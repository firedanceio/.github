# Firedance

Firedance makes FirePod, a small speaker that syncs your smart lights to the music you play.

FirePod is coming soon. Sign up for updates at [firedance.io](https://firedance.io).

## Open source

We share the tools and libraries we build along the way. New releases go up every week or so through November.

- [matter_dcl](https://github.com/firedanceio/matter_dcl): look up Matter smart home devices in the official CSA registry (Dart, [on pub.dev](https://pub.dev/packages/matter_dcl))
- [macos-audio-tap](https://github.com/firedanceio/macos-audio-tap): capture the audio your Mac is playing (Swift, [on the Swift Package Index](https://swiftpackageindex.com/firedanceio/macos-audio-tap))

Everything we've released is listed at [firedance.io/open-source](https://firedance.io/open-source/).
